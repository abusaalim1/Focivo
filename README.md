## ✨ Overview 🌿

Focivo is an Android-first focus companion for students who want to study deeper, not just longer. It combines proven focus techniques with playful motivation — a growing mascot, streaks, and friendly competition — so staying focused actually feels rewarding.

> Built for people who care about *where their attention goes*, not just how many hours they log.

## 🚀 Feature Atlas

### ⏱️ Smart Focus Timer
Pomodoro-style sessions (15m Sprint · 25m Pomodoro · 50m Deep Study · 90m Marathon) plus a fully custom timer. Two beautiful timer styles — Aurora and Classic Dial.

`Pomodoro` `Custom durations` `Pause & resume` `Session presets`

### 🛡️ Focus Shield
Optional app blocker that prevents minimizing or switching to other apps during a study session. Your focus stays locked in.

`App blocking` `Distraction tracking` `Deep focus mode`

### 📊 Insights & Analytics
Detailed breakdowns of your study patterns — total focus time, average session length, focus points, and distraction counts. Know exactly where your hours go.

`Study stats` `Progress charts` `Focus points`

### 🏆 Leaderboards & Achievements
Weekly leaderboards to compete with fellow students, plus achievements that celebrate your milestones. Studying alone doesn't have to feel lonely.

`Weekly rankings` `Achievements` `Streaks`

### 🌱 Growing Mascot
A cute companion that studies with you and grows as you focus. Because a little joy makes deep work sustainable.

`Companion` `Progress rewards`

### 🔔 Smart Alarms & Scheduling
Schedule study sessions and set alarms that respect your rhythm. Plan once, focus daily.

`Session scheduling` `Alarm studio` `Reminders`
